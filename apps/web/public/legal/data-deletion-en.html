Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 1, 2026

open333CRM is a customer service and marketing platform operated by Create Intelligens Inc. (創造智能科技股份有限公司, a company registered in Taiwan, Unified Business No. 90510433) ("we"). Businesses ("merchants") connect their own Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, LINE Official Accounts and website chat to open333CRM, and we process messages and comments on their behalf.

This page explains how to ask for your data to be deleted. It covers two groups of people:

  • A. Customers of a merchant: you sent a message to a Facebook Page, Instagram account, LINE Official Account or website chat that uses open333CRM, or commented on a post of such a Page or Instagram account.
  • B. Merchant users: you sign in to the open333CRM dashboard as an administrator or agent of a merchant.

What data we hold

GroupData
A. Customers of a merchantYour page-scoped or Instagram-scoped user ID; your public profile name and profile picture; your Instagram username, when you comment on Instagram; the messages and attachments you sent to the merchant; the text of comments that matched a keyword rule set by the merchant; the merchant's replies; and records the merchant created about you, such as tags, notes, support cases, and a phone number or email address if you gave one to the merchant.
B. Merchant usersYour name, email address, hashed password or passkey, role, and a log of the actions you took in the dashboard. When you connect a Facebook Page or Instagram account, we also store the Page or account ID, its name, and an encrypted access token.

We do not receive your Facebook or Instagram password, your friends list, or your private profile data.

A. If you are a customer of a merchant

The merchant whose Page or account you contacted decides how your data is used. You can ask for deletion in either of these ways:

  • Ask the merchant. Send a message to the Page, account or chat you contacted and ask for your data to be deleted. The merchant can submit a deletion request for your record in open333CRM.
  • Ask us. Send an email to info@aicreate360.com with the subject "Data deletion request".

When you write to us, include:

  • the name or link of the Facebook Page, Instagram account, LINE Official Account or website you contacted;
  • the profile name (and Instagram username, if any) you used;
  • the approximate date of your last message or comment.

We do not ask for your password or any identity document. Before we delete anything, we confirm that the request comes from you: we ask you to send a short confirmation message from the same Facebook, Instagram or LINE account to the Page or account you contacted. If you used website chat, which has no account, or you can no longer use your account, we work with the merchant to confirm your identity another way, for example from the phone number or email address you gave the merchant. If we cannot find your record from this information, we will reply and ask for more details, for example a screenshot of the conversation.

What happens next

  • We confirm receipt within 3 business days.
  • We locate your record, confirm your identity as described above, notify the merchant, and complete the deletion within 30 days of receiving your request.
  • We reply by email when the deletion is complete.

What is deleted

By default we carry out a full deletion of your contact record:

  • your contact profile (name, profile picture, phone, email, tags and custom attributes);
  • your page-scoped or Instagram-scoped ID and any link between your accounts on different channels;
  • your conversations, including the messages you sent and the merchant's replies;
  • images, videos and files you sent, and the stored copy of your profile picture;
  • support cases, satisfaction ratings, loyalty points and campaign form answers linked to you;
  • for comments: your commenter ID, name, username and comment text in our keyword-reply logs. We keep only a record that a reply was sent to a comment ID, with no name or text, so that the same comment does not trigger a second message.

Where a merchant has a legal duty to keep records of a transaction, the merchant may ask us to anonymize your record instead. In that case we remove your name, picture, contact details, IDs, the content of the messages you sent and the files you sent, and keep the remaining case and conversation records without anything that identifies you.

What is not deleted automatically

  • Link-click logs, broadcast delivery records and outgoing webhook delivery logs are stored separately from your contact record. Where such a record can be linked to you, we delete it by hand within the same period.
  • Data export files that a merchant generated before your request are deleted automatically 7 days after they were created.
  • Your original comments and messages on Facebook, Instagram and LINE stay on those services. You can delete them there yourself. We cannot delete content from your Facebook or Instagram account.

B. If you are a merchant user

  • Delete one customer's data. A user with the "data erase" permission can submit a deletion request for a contact through the open333CRM API. If you need help, email info@aicreate360.com and we will carry it out for you.
  • Delete a team member's account. A merchant administrator can permanently delete a team member from Settings > Team. The account and its email address are removed. Past audit entries keep the member's name and email as a record of who performed each action.
  • Disconnect a Facebook Page or Instagram account. Deactivate or delete the channel in Settings > Channels. For a Page connected with Facebook Login, deleting the channel also unsubscribes our app from that Page, and we stop receiving its events. You can also remove open333CRM under Facebook Settings > Business Integrations. A channel that already has conversations cannot be deleted in the dashboard. It can only be deactivated: we then stop processing its events, and the stored conversations and the encrypted access token remain until you ask us to delete them by email to info@aicreate360.com. Disconnecting stops new data from arriving. It does not delete conversations that were already received. To delete those, follow the next step.
  • Close your merchant account and delete all its data. The account owner sends an email from the registered address to info@aicreate360.com. We confirm the request with the owner, then delete the merchant's contacts, conversations, messages, files, channel credentials and user accounts. Before deletion, you can ask us for an export of your contacts, conversations, messages and cases.

Exceptions

We may keep limited data when the law requires it, for example invoices and payment records, or when it is needed to resolve a dispute or prevent abuse. We tell you if this applies to your request.
