open333CRM Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

open333CRM is a customer service and marketing platform for businesses. It is operated by Create Intelligens Inc. (創造智能科技股份有限公司, a company registered in Taiwan, Unified Business No. 90510433), 3F., No. 159, Sec. 1, Jiuzong Rd., Neihu Dist., Taipei City, Taiwan ("we", "us"). On Meta platforms our app is named "創造智能" (App ID 671495162407854).

A business that uses open333CRM (a "merchant") connects its own Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, LINE Official Account or website chat to the service. We then receive and send messages on that merchant's behalf, show them in one inbox, and run the automations the merchant sets up.

This policy covers two groups of people:

GroupWho you areOur role
Merchant usersAdministrators and agents who sign in to the open333CRM dashboardWe are the controller of your account data.
Customers of a merchantPeople who message a merchant's Page or account, or comment on its postsThe merchant is the controller. It decides why and how your data is used. We are a processor and handle the data on the merchant's instructions.

If you are a customer of a merchant, the merchant's own privacy policy also applies to you. This policy explains what happens to your data inside open333CRM.

This policy does not cover Facebook, Instagram, LINE or other services themselves. Their operators have their own privacy policies.

3. How we use data

We use data only for these purposes:

  • To provide the service to the merchant. We show incoming messages in the merchant's inbox, deliver the merchant's replies, run keyword rules and automations, create contact records and support cases, and produce reports for the merchant.
  • To send messages the merchant decides to send. A merchant can send messages to its own contacts, within the rules of each channel. The merchant chooses the recipients and the content.
  • To run AI features the merchant turns on. See section 5.
  • To manage accounts. We sign users in, send service emails such as invitations and password resets, and measure plan usage.
  • To keep the service secure and working. We investigate errors, prevent abuse, and keep audit records.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use customers' data for our own marketing. We do not use it to build profiles of people across different merchants: each merchant's data is kept separate, and one merchant cannot see another merchant's contacts or conversations.

Within a single merchant, the merchant can link the records of the same person on different channels, for example when that person gives the same phone number or email address on two channels.

4. How We Use Meta Platform Data

This section describes all data we receive from Facebook and Instagram through Meta's APIs and webhooks ("Meta Platform Data").

4.1 Our commitments

  • We use Meta Platform Data only to provide the features the merchant asked for: the shared inbox, replies, comment keyword replies, and the related contact records and reports.
  • We do not sell, license or rent Meta Platform Data.
  • We do not use it for advertising. We do not use it to target ads, build advertising audiences, or send it to advertising networks.
  • We do not build profiles of people across merchants, and we do not combine one merchant's Meta Platform Data with another merchant's.
  • We do not give it to data brokers, and we do not give it to any third party except the service providers listed in section 8, who may use it only on our instructions.
  • We read data only from Pages and Instagram accounts that a merchant has connected and authorized.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and are used only to call Meta's APIs for that merchant.
  • We comply with the Meta Platform Terms and the Meta Developer Policies. If Meta asks us to delete Meta Platform Data, we will delete it.

4.3 What we do not do with Meta Platform Data

  • We do not read Page insights, follower lists, or the personal Facebook profile of the admin beyond what Facebook Login needs to list the admin's Pages.
  • We do not read comments through the Graph API. Comments reach us only through webhooks for connected Pages and accounts.
  • We do not edit, hide or delete comments, and we do not create posts.
  • We do not send Meta Platform Data to other merchants, advertisers or data brokers.
  • AI processing of Meta Platform Data is described in section 5.

4.4 Deleting Meta Platform Data

  • When a merchant disconnects a Page or account. The merchant can delete or deactivate the channel in the dashboard. Deleting a Facebook Page that was connected with Facebook Login removes the stored access token and unsubscribes our app from the Page, so we stop receiving its events. A channel that already has conversations cannot be deleted in the dashboard. It can be deactivated: we then stop processing its events, and the stored conversations and the encrypted token remain until the merchant asks us to delete them.
  • When a merchant removes our app in Facebook or Instagram settings. Meta stops sending us new data and the token stops working. Data already received is deleted when the merchant asks.
  • When a customer asks. You can ask the merchant, or write to us at info@aicreate360.com. We erase your contact record, your IDs, your messages and files, the stored copy of your profile picture, and your commenter ID, name and comment text in the trigger log. The full steps are in our Data Deletion Instructions.
  • When a merchant closes its account. The account owner asks us by email to close the account. After we confirm the request with the owner, we delete the merchant's Meta Platform Data together with its other data. See section 7.
  • Content on Facebook and Instagram itself, such as your original comment, stays there. You can delete it in those apps.

5. AI processing

open333CRM has AI features that a merchant can turn on, such as automatic replies based on the merchant's knowledge base, conversation summaries, and classification of conversations.

  • What is sent. When a merchant uses an AI feature, the relevant conversation text and extracts from the merchant's knowledge base are sent to an AI model to produce the result. A conversation summary request can include the customer's display name. Messages from Facebook and Instagram are included when the conversation takes place on those channels.
  • Which providers. The service supports Google's Gemini API, and models that we host ourselves. The merchant, or we on its behalf, chooses which is used. A merchant may use its own Gemini API key, in which case Google processes the data under the merchant's own agreement with Google. The providers in use are listed in section 8: the Google Gemini API, and open-source models we run on our own servers (Ollama).
  • Masking of personal data. For merchants that have the data protection option enabled, message text is first sent to a de-identification service that replaces personal details such as names and phone numbers with labels. The AI model then reads only the masked copy. We ask this service not to retain the text, and we do not keep its mapping back to the original values. If the service is unavailable, an incoming message is saved without a masked copy and the AI model does not read it. This option is not enabled for every merchant. The de-identification service is operated by us or one of our affiliated companies.
  • What we keep. For each AI request we keep a usage record: provider, model, feature, token counts, cost and the related conversation. This record does not contain the message text.
  • Model training. We do not use Meta Platform Data or customers' messages to train or fine-tune AI models. How an AI provider handles the data it receives is governed by that provider's terms of service; when a merchant uses its own key, it is governed by the merchant's agreement with the provider.
  • Human review. AI replies are sent in the merchant's name. The merchant decides when AI replies are used and can hand a conversation over to a human agent.

9. International transfers

Our servers are located in Amazon Web Services (AWS), US West (Oregon, us-west-2) region. Some providers in section 8, and Meta and LINE themselves, process data in other countries, including the United States. Where we transfer personal data abroad, we use providers bound by contract to protect it, and we follow the restrictions on international transfers set by Taiwan's competent authorities.

10. Security

  • Access tokens for connected channels and API keys are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
  • Each merchant's data is separated at both the application level and the database level (row-level security), so one merchant cannot read another's data.
  • Access inside a merchant is controlled by roles and permissions that the merchant assigns.
  • Passwords are stored as hashes. Passkeys are supported.
  • Incoming webhooks from Meta and LINE are verified by signature before they are processed.
  • Connections to the service use HTTPS.
  • Important actions in the dashboard are recorded in an audit log.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify the merchant concerned and, where the law requires, you and the competent authority.

11. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask to: check or review your personal data; receive a copy; have it supplemented or corrected; have collection, processing or use stopped; and have it deleted. Where the GDPR applies, you also have the rights to restriction, objection and data portability, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority.

If you are a customer of a merchant, the quickest way is to ask the merchant, because the merchant controls your data. You can also write to us at info@aicreate360.com. Tell us which Page or account you contacted and the profile name you used. We will pass your request to the merchant and help carry it out. We do not ask for your password or an identity document. Before we delete data, we ask you to confirm the request from the same Facebook or Instagram account you used to contact the merchant.

If you are a merchant user, ask your organization's administrator, or write to us at info@aicreate360.com.

We answer within 30 days. We do not charge for these requests.

How to request deletion, and exactly what is deleted, is described in our Data Deletion Instructions.

13. Children

open333CRM is a tool for businesses. Merchant user accounts are for adults acting for a business. We do not knowingly collect data from children for our own purposes. A merchant's customers must meet the minimum age set by Facebook, Instagram or LINE to use those services. If you believe a child's data has been processed through our service, contact the merchant or us and we will help delete it.
